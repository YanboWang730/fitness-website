肩部训练动作 Shoulder Exercises

哑铃坐姿推肩 Seated Dumbbell Shoulder Press

[图：坐姿哑铃推肩，三角肌中束+前束红色高亮 | Illustration: anterior & medial delts highlighted]

目标肌群：三角肌前束、中束（整体肩膀）Target: Anterior & Medial Deltoids

动作说明：坐在凳上，腰背贴实，哑铃举到耳朵两侧，掌心朝前，垂直向上推起，顶点不要锁死肘关节，缓慢下放。

Instruction: Sit upright, dumbbells at ear level. Press straight up, avoid locking elbows, lower with control.

哑铃前平举 Dumbbell Front Raise

[图：哑铃前平举，三角肌前束红色高亮 | Illustration: anterior delts highlighted]

目标肌群：三角肌前束 Target: Anterior Deltoids

动作说明：站姿，手臂自然下垂，手肘微弯，哑铃向前抬到与肩平齐，顶峰稍停，匀速下放，不要甩动借力。

Instruction: Stand with slight elbow bend. Raise dumbbells forward to shoulder height, pause, lower slowly, no swinging.

哑铃俯身飞鸟 Bent-Over Dumbbell Reverse Fly

[图：俯身飞鸟，三角肌后束红色高亮 | Illustration: posterior delts highlighted]

目标肌群：三角肌后束、上背 Target: Posterior Deltoids, Upper Back

动作说明：髋部折叠俯身，背部平直，手臂微屈，向两侧打开哑铃，感受肩膀后侧收紧，缓慢还原。

Instruction: Hinge at hips, flat back, soft elbows. Open dumbbells outwards, squeeze rear shoulder, return slowly.

史密斯推肩 Smith Machine Shoulder Press

[图：史密斯推肩，三角肌前束、中束红色高亮 | Illustration: anterior & medial delts highlighted]

目标肌群：三角肌前束、中束 Target: Anterior & Medial Deltoids

动作说明：座椅调好高度，杠铃落在锁骨上方，竖直向上推起，轨迹稳定，适合新手找肩部发力、控制重量。

Instruction: Adjust seat, bar at upper chest. Press vertically. Fixed path is friendly for beginners to master form.

哑铃侧平举 Dumbbell Lateral Raise

[图：哑铃侧平举，三角肌中束红色高亮 | Illustration: medial delts highlighted]

目标肌群：三角肌中束（加宽肩膀） Target: Medial Deltoids (Shoulder width)

动作说明：站姿，手肘保持微弯固定，哑铃向两侧抬至肩高，想象往远泼水，不要抬太高超过耳朵，慢放。

Instruction: Stand, keep elbows slightly bent. Raise dumbbells sideways to shoulder level, avoid lifting above ears.

绳索面拉 Cable Face Pull

[图：绳索面拉，三角肌后束、肩袖、上斜方下束红色高亮 | Illustration: rear delts & rotator cuff highlighted]

目标肌群：三角肌后束、肩袖肌群、中下斜方肌 Target: Posterior Delts, Rotator Cuff, Mid-lower Traps

动作说明：高位绳索，拉绳索向额头/面部，手肘向外打开，肩胛骨后缩收紧，这个动作对改善圆肩很友好。

Instruction: High pulley. Pull rope toward forehead, flare elbows out, squeeze shoulder blades. Great for rounded shoulder posture.
