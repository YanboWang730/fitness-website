手臂训练动作 Arm Exercises

绳索下压 Rope Tricep Pushdown

[图：绳索下压，肱三头肌红色高亮 | Illustration: triceps highlighted]

目标肌群：肱三头肌 Target: Triceps Brachii

动作说明：龙门架装上绳索把手，大臂贴紧身体不动，小臂向下压，顶峰收紧三头，缓慢放回。

Instruction: Attach rope to cable. Keep upper arms tight to torso, push forearm down, squeeze triceps and return slowly.

单手绳索臂屈伸 Single Arm Cable Tricep Extension

[图：单手绳索臂屈伸，肱三头肌红色高亮 | Illustration: triceps highlighted]

目标肌群：肱三头肌 Target: Triceps Brachii

动作说明：单手握住高位绳索，手肘朝上固定，小臂向前伸展，感受单侧三头充分拉伸收缩。

Instruction: Grip high cable with one hand, elbow pointing up. Extend forearm forward to stretch and contract triceps.

仰卧杠铃臂屈伸 Lying Barbell Tricep Extension

[图：仰卧杠铃臂屈伸，肱三头肌红色高亮 | Illustration: triceps highlighted]

目标肌群：肱三头肌长头 Target: Long head of Triceps

动作说明：平躺卧推凳，杠铃在头顶上方，只活动肘关节，缓慢向额头下放再伸直推起。

Instruction: Lie flat on bench, bar over head. Only bend elbow, lower bar toward forehead then extend back up.

锤式弯举 Hammer Curl

[图：锤式弯举，肱肌、肱桡肌红色高亮 | Illustration: brachialis & brachioradialis highlighted]

目标肌群：肱肌、肱桡肌 Target: Brachialis & Brachioradialis

动作说明：双手握哑铃，掌心相对，大臂贴紧身体，向上弯举，顶峰停顿，匀速下放。

Instruction: Hold dumbbells with palms facing each other. Keep upper arm fixed, curl up, squeeze and lower slowly.

牧师凳弯举 Preacher Curl

[图：牧师凳弯举，肱二头肌红色高亮 | Illustration: biceps brachii highlighted]

目标肌群：肱二头肌 Target: Biceps Brachii

动作说明：手臂搭在牧师凳斜面上，大臂固定，弯举哑铃/杠铃，全程控制，减少借力。

Instruction: Rest arms on preacher pad, fix upper arm. Curl weight upward with controlled movement, no swinging.

坐姿绳索二头弯举 Seated Cable Bicep Curl

[图：坐姿绳索二头弯举，肱二头肌红色高亮 | Illustration: biceps brachii highlighted]

目标肌群：肱二头肌 Target: Biceps Brachii

动作说明：坐姿，低位绳索把手，大臂贴紧身体，向上弯举，绳索持续保持张力，收缩感更强。

Instruction: Sit down, grip low cable handle. Upper arm stationary, curl up. Cable provides constant tension on biceps.
