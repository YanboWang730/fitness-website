背部训练动作 Back Exercises

引体向上 Pull-Up

[图：引体向上，背阔肌红色高亮 | Illustration: latissimus dorsi highlighted]

目标肌群：背阔肌、大圆肌、肱二头肌、上背 Target: Lats, Teres Major, Biceps, Upper Back

动作说明：双手宽握单杠，挺胸沉肩，拉动身体向上直到下巴过杠，缓慢下放，全程不要耸肩。

Instruction: Wide grip, chest up, shoulders down. Pull until chin over bar, lower slowly, avoid shrugging shoulders.

宽握高位下拉 Wide-Grip Lat Pulldown

[图：高位下拉，背阔外侧红色高亮 | Illustration: outer lats highlighted]

目标肌群：背阔肌外侧、大圆肌 Target: Outer Lats, Teres Major

动作说明：坐于器械，固定大腿，握杆略宽于肩，沉肩，把横杆拉至上胸位置，控制回放不要借力甩动。

Instruction: Sit and lock thighs, wide grip, pull bar to upper chest, control the return without swinging.

坐姿绳索划船 Seated Cable Row

[图：坐姿划船，中背菱形肌红色高亮 | Illustration: rhomboids highlighted]

目标肌群：菱形肌、斜方肌中下束、中背、背阔中部 Target: Rhomboids, Mid-Lower Traps, Mid-Back

动作说明：脚踩踏板，屈膝，腰背挺直，把手柄拉向下腹部，肩胛骨向后收紧，缓慢放回。

Instruction: Feet on platform, flat back. Pull handle toward lower belly, squeeze shoulder blades together, return slowly.

直臂下压 Straight Arm Pulldown

[图：直臂下压，背阔下沿红色高亮 | Illustration: lower lats highlighted]

目标肌群：背阔肌下沿（孤立背阔） Target: Lower Lats (Isolation)

动作说明：微屈手肘固定角度，不要弯胳膊，用背阔发力把横杆向下压至大腿前侧，缓慢放回，重点感受背阔拉长收缩。

Instruction: Keep elbows slightly bent and fixed. Pull bar down with lats only, focus on stretch and contraction of latissimus dorsi.
