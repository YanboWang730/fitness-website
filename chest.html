胸部训练动作 Chest Exercises

平板杠铃卧推 Barbell Flat Bench Press

[图：平板杠铃卧推，全胸红色高亮 | Illustration: full chest highlighted]

目标肌群：整体胸部（全胸）Target: Entire Chest

动作说明：平躺卧推凳，双脚踩实地面，握杠铃略宽于肩，缓慢下放至中下胸位置，发力推起，不要锁死肘关节。

Instruction: Lie flat on bench, feet firmly on ground. Grip bar slightly wider than shoulder. Lower slowly to mid-lower chest, push up without locking elbows.

上斜杠铃卧推 Barbell Incline Bench Press

[图：上斜杠铃卧推，上胸红色高亮 | Illustration: upper chest highlighted]

目标肌群：上胸 Target: Upper Chest

动作说明：凳面30°左右倾斜，杠铃下放到上胸锁骨下方，感受上胸发力，控制下放速度。

Instruction: Bench incline ~30°. Lower bar to upper chest below collarbone, control speed and focus on upper chest contraction.

低位绳索夹胸 Low Cable Crossover

[图：低位绳索夹胸，胸外侧红色高亮 | Illustration: outer chest highlighted]

目标肌群：胸外侧沿 Target: Outer Chest

动作说明：龙门架低位把手，身体微微前倾，手臂微屈，从下向内上方夹拢，顶峰收缩停顿一下。

Instruction: Use low pulleys, lean slightly forward, keep elbows soft. Bring handles up & inward, squeeze chest at peak contraction.

哑铃推胸 Dumbbell Bench Press

[图：哑铃推胸，胸中缝红色高亮 | Illustration: chest midline highlighted]

目标肌群：胸中缝、全胸 Target: Chest Midline, Entire Chest

动作说明：平板凳，哑铃下放幅度比杠铃更大，两侧向中间靠拢，强化胸部中间位置。

Instruction: On flat bench, dumbbells have a larger range of motion, squeeze inward to build chest separation.

坐姿推胸器械 Seated Chest Press Machine

[图：坐姿推胸，中胸红色高亮 | Illustration: mid-chest highlighted]

目标肌群：中胸 Target: Mid Chest

动作说明：背部贴紧靠背，把手对齐胸部高度，向前推出，匀速放回，稳定性高，适合找发力感。

Instruction: Keep back pressed against pad, handles aligned with chest. Push forward and return slowly, great for beginners.

双杠臂屈伸 Dips

[图：双杠臂屈伸，下胸红色高亮 | Illustration: lower chest highlighted]

目标肌群：下胸、三头肌 Target: Lower Chest, Triceps

动作说明：身体微微前倾，重心靠前，缓慢下放，用下胸发力撑起；身体直立会更多练三头。

Instruction: Lean forward to emphasize lower chest. Keep controlled descent. Upright posture shifts focus to triceps.
