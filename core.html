核心训练动作 Core Exercises

卷腹 Crunch

[图：卷腹，上腹红色高亮 | Illustration: upper abs highlighted]

目标肌群：上腹 Target: Upper Abdominals

动作说明：屈膝平躺，肩胛骨抬离地面，不要整个人坐起，感受上腹收缩。

Instruction: Lie supine with knees bent. Lift shoulder blades off the floor, focus on upper abs contraction.

悬垂举腿 Hanging Leg Raise

[图：悬垂举腿，下腹红色高亮 | Illustration: lower abs highlighted]

目标肌群：下腹 Target: Lower Abdominals

动作说明：双手抓单杠悬空，双腿伸直抬起至与地面平行，缓慢下放，避免甩动借力。

Instruction: Hang from a pull-up bar. Raise straight legs to horizontal, lower slowly, avoid swinging.

俄罗斯转体 Russian Twist

[图：俄罗斯转体，腹斜肌红色高亮 | Illustration: obliques highlighted]

目标肌群：腹斜肌（侧腰） Target: Obliques

动作说明：上半身后倾，双脚离地，双手合十左右转动躯干，挤压侧腰。

Instruction: Lean back, lift feet off ground, rotate torso side to side, squeeze obliques.

绳索卷腹 Cable Crunch

[图：绳索卷腹，上腹红色高亮 | Illustration: upper abs highlighted]

目标肌群：上腹 Target: Upper Abdominals

动作说明：跪在器械前，双手拉住绳索，用腹部发力把身体向下卷，缓慢回位。

Instruction: Kneel facing cable machine. Pull the rope down by crunching abs, return slowly.

仰卧举腿 Supine Leg Raise

[图：仰卧举腿，下腹红色高亮 | Illustration: lower abs highlighted]

目标肌群：下腹 Target: Lower Abdominals

动作说明：平躺地面，双手垫在臀部下方，双腿伸直抬起再缓慢下放，脚后跟不要落地。

Instruction: Lie flat, hands under hips. Raise straight legs, lower slowly, keep heels off floor.
